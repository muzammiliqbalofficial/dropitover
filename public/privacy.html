Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

At DropItOver (accessible from https://dropitover.com/), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by DropItOver and how we use it.

1. Zero-Knowledge File Sharing

DropItOver is designed with a privacy-first architecture:

  • Direct Wi-Fi & Room Shares: Use encrypted peer-to-peer WebRTC data channels. Your files travel directly browser-to-browser and never touch our servers.
  • Link Shares: Files uploaded via links are encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM before storage, and are permanently deleted immediately upon expiration or after download.

2. Log Files & Analytics

DropItOver follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

3. Cookies & Third-Party Advertisers

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to dropitover.com and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

We do not sell personal data. We do not require user accounts or email addresses to use our service.

5. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@dropitover.com.
