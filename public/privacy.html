Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

DropItOver is engineered from the ground up to respect and protect user privacy. This policy provides complete, transparent disclosure regarding data handling, cryptographic protections, advertising cookies, and user rights under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

1. Summary of Data Practices

  • No Account Required: No usernames, passwords, phone numbers, or credit cards are ever collected.
  • Zero-Server P2P Mode: Files transferred via local Wi-Fi or Room mode flow directly browser-to-browser via WebRTC and never touch our servers.
  • Encrypted Ephemeral Link Mode: Files uploaded for link sharing are encrypted client-side/edge using AES-256-GCM and automatically deleted upon expiration.
  • No First-Party Tracking: We do not deploy invasive analytics trackers or behavioral profiling tools.

2. Data Processing by Transfer Mode

Transfer Mode Data Transmission Path Server Retention
Devices on Wi-Fi (P2P) Direct browser-to-browser WebRTC DataChannel (DTLS-SRTP). 0 Seconds (Never stored)
Multi-Device Rooms Direct peer mesh relayed through ephemeral signaling WebSocket. 0 Seconds (Never stored)
Encrypted Share Links Encrypted binary chunks uploaded to Cloudflare R2 object cache. Deleted on expiry (1 hr – 7 days) or instant burn.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising Disclosures

DropItOver uses Google AdSense to serve non-intrusive advertisements on editorial and content pages. Google and third-party advertising vendors use cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) and web beacons to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website and other websites across the internet.

  • Personalized Ads Opt-Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Network Advertising Initiative: You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info/choices or youradchoices.com.
  • Partner Site Privacy Notice: To understand how Google manages data in its advertising products, please review How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

4. Local Storage & Cookie Usage

DropItOver itself does not set persistent tracking cookies. We utilize browser localStorage solely to remember your chosen device display nickname locally on your device for peer discovery convenience. This information is never transmitted to analytics databases.

5. Infrastructure & Third-Party Service Providers

DropItOver is hosted globally on Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers, R2 Storage, Durable Objects, and TURN relays). Cloudflare acts as a data processor adhering to strict Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and ISO 27001 certifications.

6. GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Compliance

Under GDPR and CCPA:

  • Right of Access & Portability: Because we hold no user accounts and do not track IP addresses beyond ephemeral rate-limiting counters, we do not possess personally identifiable records to export.
  • Right to Erasure: Senders of link shares can manually trigger immediate deletion ("Delete Now") at any time before link expiration.
  • Non-Sale of Personal Data: DropItOver does not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

DropItOver does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13.
