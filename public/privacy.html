Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

DropItOver lets you send files and text between devices. This page explains exactly what is stored, for how long, and what never reaches us at all. It is written to match how the service actually works rather than to sound reassuring.

1. The short version

  • No account, no email address, no password. Nothing identifies you to us.
  • Files sent to a nearby device or inside a room never reach our servers.
  • Files sent as a link are stored, encrypted, and deleted when they expire.
  • We run no analytics and load no third-party scripts on the transfer pages.

2. What happens to your files

The service works three ways, and they are not equivalent for privacy. This distinction matters more than anything else on this page.

How you sent itWhere the file goes
Devices on your Wi-Fi Directly from one browser to the other over an encrypted connection. The file never reaches our servers and there is nothing for us to store, read or delete.
Room The same: browser to browser. Our server only introduces the two devices to each other.
Share link Uploaded to our storage, because the recipient may open it hours later. Encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it is written, and deleted on expiry.

3. How link shares are encrypted, precisely

Your file is uploaded in pieces. Each piece is encrypted with AES-256-GCM on our server, as it arrives, before it is written to storage. Each file gets its own key, derived from a master secret we hold, and each piece gets its own initialisation vector.

To be clear about what this does and does not mean: encryption protects your file against anyone who obtains the stored data without our key, including our storage provider. It is not end-to-end encryption, and it is not zero-knowledge. We hold the key, so we are technically capable of decrypting a stored file. We do not do so in the ordinary course of running the service, but you should know it is possible.

If that matters for what you are sending, use the Wi-Fi or room modes instead. In those, the file genuinely never reaches us.

4. What we store, and for how long

DataWhyKept for
Encrypted file contents (link shares only) So the recipient can download later Until expiry: 1 hour to 7 days, your choice, 24 hours by default. Sooner if you enable delete-after-download or delete it yourself.
File names, sizes, types, and any message you typed To show the recipient what they are getting Deleted with the share
A one-way hash of your IP address To group devices on the same network, and to enforce the daily upload limit Up to 2 days for the limit counter. The address itself is never stored.
The display name you choose So other devices can tell which one is yours Stored in your own browser only. It never leaves your device except to be shown to devices you are transferring with.
Room membership To connect people in the same room The room record expires 12 hours after the last person joins

Deletion is automatic: a scheduled job removes expired shares every ten minutes, and an expired link stops working the moment it is opened.

5. What we do not collect

  • No accounts, email addresses or passwords.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No profiles, no selling of data, and no sharing with anyone except the providers listed below.
  • We do not read your files or your messages.

6. Cookies and advertising

DropItOver itself sets no cookies. It stores your chosen device name in your browser's local storage, which stays on your device.

This site may display advertising supplied by Google. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, and read how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Third-party vendors may also be opted out of at aboutads.info/choices.

7. Service providers

The service runs on Cloudflare, which processes requests and stores encrypted link-share files on our behalf. Connections between devices may use a relay operated by Cloudflare when a direct connection is impossible; relayed traffic stays encrypted end to end and the relay cannot read it. Advertising, where shown, is supplied by Google.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no account and nothing that identifies you, there is normally no personal data of yours for us to look up, export or erase. If you created a share link, you can delete it yourself at any time from the page where you created it, or simply let it expire. If you believe we hold data about you and want it removed, contact us and we will act on it.

Where the GDPR or CCPA applies, our lawful basis for the limited processing described above is our legitimate interest in operating and protecting the service. We do not sell personal information.

9. Children

DropItOver is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Because no account is required, we cannot identify a user's age.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be summarised here rather than made silently.

11. Contact

Questions, or a request to remove something: support@dropitover.com.
