Updates

What's changed

A running log of what's new and what got fixed, in the order it happened. Nothing here is a marketing announcement; most of these entries exist because something didn't work the way it should have, and this says plainly what changed and why.

19 August 2026

Rooms officially support up to 8 people

The landing page used to describe rooms as "you and someone else," which undersold what they actually did. Rooms have always supported group sessions; the page now says so, and shows how many of the 8 slots are filled while you're in one. If a room shows as full, someone else joining a moment earlier used the last slot; wait for one to leave, or start a second room.

11 August 2026

Live on dropitover.com

Moved off the temporary workers.dev address onto a proper domain, with a certificate issued automatically. Plain http:// and the www. address now redirect to the canonical https address instead of quietly serving a duplicate copy of the site.

10 August 2026

Cross-network rooms actually connect

Two devices on different networks, especially a phone on mobile data, often couldn't find a direct path to each other and sat at "Connecting…" indefinitely. Added a relay for exactly that case, reachable even on restrictive networks, so a phone on mobile data can now reliably join a room with a laptop on Wi-Fi. Connections that still can't reach each other now say so, with a concrete next step, instead of spinning forever.

9 August 2026

Rebuilt to run on Cloudflare's edge

Replaced the original server with one built directly on Cloudflare Workers, R2 and D1: no server to patch, no container, nothing to keep running. Link shares are chunked into 8MB pieces during upload and encrypted piece by piece, which is what makes a 2GB file possible at all under the platform's per-request size limit.

9 August 2026

Three modes, one page

Same-network discovery, expiring links, and multi-person rooms shipped together as the initial release, rather than adding them one at a time behind a paywall. All three are free with no file count limit, which was the whole reason for building this instead of using an existing tool.

Found something that doesn't belong on this page, meaning a bug rather than a feature? Say so at support@dropitover.com and it'll get looked at.
