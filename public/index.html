        Frequently Asked Questions

        How do I transfer files between Android, Windows, iPhone, and Mac?
        Open dropitover.com on both devices. On the same Wi-Fi network, devices discover each other automatically under "Devices on your Wi-Fi". Select a file to send instantly. For remote devices, click "Get a link".
        Is DropItOver free and what is the file size limit?
        DropItOver is 100% free with no account creation or subscription required. You can send files up to 2 GB per file, with no limit on how many files you send at once. A single share link can total up to 8 GB.
        Are my transfers secure and private?
        Direct Wi-Fi transfers and room shares use peer-to-peer WebRTC encryption, meaning your files pass directly between devices without ever reaching a server. Link shares are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before storage.
        How is DropItOver an alternative to Apple AirDrop?
        AirDrop is restricted to Apple hardware. DropItOver brings AirDrop-like simplicity to all operating systems: Android, Windows, Linux, macOS, and iOS, directly in your browser.
        Files you send to nearby devices or inside a room go straight across, so nothing is kept.
        Links are locked and deleted the moment they run out.
